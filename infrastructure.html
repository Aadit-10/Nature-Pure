Advanced Manufacturing Infrastructure & Technical Capabilities

At Nature Pure Extracts, our infrastructure reflects a strong commitment to technological excellence, precision manufacturing, quality assurance, and sustainable operations. Developed to meet the evolving requirements of global food, nutraceutical, cosmetic, pharmaceutical, and specialty ingredient industries, our facilities integrate advanced extraction technologies with robust process controls and international quality standards.

With decades of manufacturing expertise and continuous investment in innovation, we have established a modern production environment capable of delivering high-purity natural ingredients with consistency, scalability, and reliability.

Supercritical CO₂ Extraction Facility

Our core manufacturing strength lies in advanced Supercritical CO₂ Extraction Technology, enabling efficient extraction of sensitive bioactive compounds while preserving their natural composition, functionality, and purity.

The facility is equipped with:

  • 2 x 50 Litre extraction systems designed to operate up to 400 bar pressure
  • 2 x 125 Litre extraction systems designed to operate up to 200 bar pressure
  • Integrated entrainer pump systems for co-solvent introduction and specialized extraction applications
  • Advanced process monitoring and control systems
  • Flexible pilot-scale and commercial-scale processing capabilities

These systems enable the precise extraction and processing of botanica materials, nutraceutical ingredients, oleoresins, specialty extracts, and value-added natural actives across diverse applications.

Processing & Manufacturing Capabilities

Our infrastructure is designed to support technically sophisticated manufacturing operations with an annual raw material processing capacity exceeding 150 tons.

The facility supports the production of:

  • Botanical and herbal extracts
  • Nutraceutical and wellness ingredients
  • Specialty natural actives
  • Food, cosmetic, and pharmaceutical colours
  • Specialty blends and lakes
  • Calcium and sodium phosphates
  • Sodium citrate
  • Meta Hydroxy Benzaldehyde and allied specialty ingredients

Our manufacturing systems are engineered to ensure process consistency, product integrity, operational efficiency, and compliance with stringent quality requirements.

Research, Development & Process Innovation

Innovation and process development remain integral to our operational philosophy. Our infrastructure supports continuous research and technical advancement focused on:

  • Customized extraction solutions
  • Process optimization and scale-up
  • Product standardization
  • New ingredient development
  • Application-oriented formulation support
  • Pilot-to-commercial manufacturing transition

Our expertise in advanced extraction technologies enables us to develop tailored solutions aligned with evolving industry and customer requirements.

Quality Assurance & Analytical Capabilities

Quality is embedded throughout every stage of our operations. Our in-house Quality Assurance and Quality Control systems are supported by advanced analytical infrastructure to ensure product safety, purity, consistency, and regulatory compliance.

Our analytical capabilities include:

  • Trace metal analysis
  • Raw material and finished product evaluation
  • In-process quality monitoring
  • Batch traceability and documentation
  • Stability and specification testing

Key analytical instrumentation includes:

  • Metrohm Trace Metal Analyser with detection capability at PPB (Parts Per Billion) levels
  • Advanced laboratory systems for precision analytical testing and quality verification

Through comprehensive quality systems and stringent testing protocols, we ensure consistent adherence to global quality standards and customer specifications.

Utilities & Process Support Systems

The manufacturing facility is supported by robust utilities and engineered process support infrastructure designed to ensure operational reliability, safety, and efficiency.

These include:

  • Advanced water treatment systems
  • Controlled process utilities
  • Specialized support systems for high-pressure extraction operations
  • Efficient material handling and storage infrastructure
  • Process-controlled manufacturing environments

Our infrastructure is designed to support seamless operations across multiple product categories while maintaining high standards of process control and manufacturing efficiency.

Environmental Responsibility & Sustainable Operations

Sustainability and environmental responsibility are integral to our manufacturing approach. The facility incorporates comprehensive systems designed to minimize environmental impact while supporting efficient and responsible production practices.

Our environmental management infrastructure includes:

  • Comprehensive water effluent treatment systems
  • Advanced treatment technologies incorporating flocculation and cavitation processes
  • Responsible waste management systems
  • Resource-efficient operational practices
  • Environmentally conscious manufacturing initiatives

By integrating sustainable practices into our operations, we remain committed to responsible manufacturing and long-term environmental stewardship.

Safety, Compliance & Operational Excellence

Our operations are guided by stringent safety protocols, structured quality systems, and continuous improvement practices to ensure manufacturing excellence across all stages of production.

We maintain:

  • SOP-driven manufacturing systems
  • Controlled and process-oriented production environments
  • Comprehensive operational and quality management practices
  • Continuous improvement initiatives across manufacturing and infrastructure
  • Strong focus on safety, reliability, and regulatory compliance

Supported by advanced technology, experienced technical expertise, and a commitment to operational excellence, Nature Pure Extracts continues to strengthen its position as a trusted global partner for advanced natural extraction and specialty ingredient manufacturing solutions.

Precision Freeze-Drying

Equipped with a 50 kg/batch freeze-drying unit, our facility offers scalable processing from R&D pilot batches through to commercial production. Using controlled low-temperature drying under vacuum, we protect heat-sensitive fruits, vegetables, herbs, and botanicals while locking in their natural color, flavor, profile, and active benefits.

Materials Handled: Fresh fruits, vegetables, herbs, and specialized botanicals

Outputs:

Formats: Powders, flakes, cut pieces, and custom-tailored specifications

Flexibility: Custom batch processing adapted to specific raw material needs and customer requirements
