What is Supercritical CO₂ Extraction and why is it better?

Supercritical CO₂ extraction (SCFE) is an advanced technology that uses carbon dioxide under high pressure to extract active compounds from natural sources. This method ensures solvent-free extracts, preserves heat-sensitive nutrients, and produces high-quality botanical extracts with superior purity compared to conventional extraction techniques.

What are the key benefits of supercritical extraction over conventional methods?

Compared to traditional extraction, supercritical CO₂ extraction offers no solvent residue, pesticide-free extracts, consistent flavor profiles, and retention of bioactive compounds. It also ensures safer, eco-friendly processing and higher product stability, making it ideal for high-end applications.
